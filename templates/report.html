Suggested Yoga & Pranayama Protocols

{% if hypertension == 'yes' or (bp_sys|int) > 135 %}
For Hypertension & BP Control: 💰 Savings: ~₹2,800/year

Practice Anulom Vilom Pranayama (Alternate Nostril Breathing) and Shavasana (Corpse Pose) daily for 10-15 minutes.

🧬 Rationale: Alternate nostril breathing regulates the autonomic nervous system by shifting the body from a sympathetic (fight-or-flight) to a parasympathetic (rest-and-digest) state, helping reduce arterial pressure. Shavasana reduces peripheral vascular resistance and relaxes skeletal muscles, directly lowering blood pressure.
{% endif %} {% if diabetes == 'yes' or risk_diabetes == 'High Risk' %}
For Diabetes Management: 💰 Savings: ~₹3,500/year

Incorporate Mandukasana (Frog Pose) and Vakrasana (Spinal Twist) to stimulate the pancreas and liver.

🧬 Rationale: Mandukasana involves deep abdominal compression while bending forward, which physically massages intra-abdominal organs and stimulates the pancreatic beta cells to produce and secrete insulin. Vakrasana improves blood circulation to the liver and kidneys, boosting overall metabolic clearance.
{% endif %} {% if bmi|float > 25.0 %}
For Weight Control & Obesity: 💰 Savings: ~₹4,200/year

Perform Surya Namaskar (12 steps of Sun Salutation) and Kapalbhati Pranayama.

🧬 Rationale: Surya Namaskar is a dynamic full-body aerobic sequence that increases heart rate, burns calories, and recruits major skeletal muscle groups to improve core strength. Kapalbhati uses rapid abdominal exhalations to tone the abdominal muscles, improve bowel motility, and elevate the Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR).
{% endif %}
Daily Mental Resilience (Stress): 💰 Savings: ~₹1,500/year

Practice 15 minutes of Dhyana (Mindfulness Meditation) or Bhramari Pranayama (Bee Breath).

🧬 Rationale: Dhyana decreases the size of the amygdala (the brain's fear/stress center) and lowers systemic cortisol levels. Bhramari generates a low-frequency hum that vibrates the cranium, stimulating the vagus nerve and releasing nitric oxide, which instantly dilates blood vessels and calms mental chatter.
